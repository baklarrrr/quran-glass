Quran Glass: privacy and terms

Quran Glass is a free Quran reader for Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, made by Bakar Studio as a personal project. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It uses the Quran Foundation APIs (Quran.com) for the Quran text, word meanings, word audio and word timing.

What is kept, and where

  • On the glasses only, in the browser's own storage: your settings, your place (surah, ayah and word), your progress, whether a tip has been shown, and the surah list (which is fetched again after seven days). Nothing of this leaves the glasses. Removing the app from the glasses removes it.
  • The Quran text, meanings and audio are loaded while you read and are not stored by the app.

Who sees what

  • Loading the app, its fonts and its content contacts these services, which see your network address as any website would: GitHub Pages (the app), Google Fonts (the Arabic fonts), the Quran Foundation API and its audio host (text, meanings, word audio and timing), QuranicAudio (recitations) and, for reciters marked “aligned”, mp3quran.net (recitations) and Bakar Studio's timing files on GitHub Pages.
  • Follow my voice (optional, with the Tidal Glass phone app): the glasses and your phone exchange short messages through public MQTT brokers (broker.emqx.io, broker.hivemq.com and, as a spare, public.cloud.shiftr.io). Each message is sealed with a key known only to your glasses and phone, so the brokers carry unreadable data; they can see when messages pass. This web app records no audio.

Terms of use

  • Quran Glass is free for personal use. The Quran text is shown exactly as the Quran Foundation API delivers it. Gilding follows recorded timing, your own marks or what your phone hears; it does not check recitation or tajwīd.
  • Quran data provided by Quran.Foundation (Quran.com). English translation: Saheeh International. Recitations: QuranicAudio; reciters marked “aligned”: mp3quran.net, word timing by Bakar Studio (CC BY 4.0). Follow my voice uses mrmuminov's model, built on NVIDIA FastConformer (CC BY 4.0). Each keeps its own terms. Quran Glass is not affiliated with Meta, Quran Foundation, QuranicAudio or mp3quran.net.
  • The app is provided as it is, without warranty.
